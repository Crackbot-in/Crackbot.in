Crackbot is an independent, community-driven cybersecurity research group. We conduct research on vulnerabilities and malware, publish technical analysis, develop open-source security tooling, and share actionable threat intelligence with the defender community.

This site is our public home: a place for published research, tool releases, and technical write-ups. Everything we release under the Crackbot name follows one rule set — ethics before exploitation, evidence over assumptions, and coordinated responsible disclosure.

## What to expect

When research is ready, we publish it here: CVE root-cause analysis, malware behavior deep-dives, forensics walkthroughs, and tooling write-ups. We will not publish fluff — every finding must satisfy our validation gates before it goes out:

- Detection and mitigation guidance included
- Mitigation steps documented
- Reproducible artifacts (PCAPs, dumps, logs)
- Vendor coordination proof, where applicable
- Peer review within the team

## Where we work in the open

Our tooling and research artifacts live [on GitHub](https://github.com/Crackbot-in). Repositories go public as soon as they are safe to share. Threat intelligence feeds and advisories will be published through this site as the platform matures.

## How to reach us

For general questions, email **system@crackbot.in**. For support, use **support@crackbot.in**. If you are a vendor who has received a report from us, reply directly — our disclosures include coordination timelines and safe-harbor language.
