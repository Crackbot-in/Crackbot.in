Crackbot is a small group of people who work in cyber security. We started it because we wanted to give something back to the community we learned from.

## What we'll publish

When we have something worth sharing, it goes here. That could be:

- write-ups of vulnerabilities, after the vendor has been told and had time to fix them;
- notes from malware we have analysed;
- threat activity we have been following;
- forensics and incident response methods.

We won't post on a schedule. We would rather publish less and get it right.

## How we work

We test systems we own or have permission to test. If we find a vulnerability, including a zero-day, we report it privately first. The details are in our [disclosure policy](/disclosure/).

## Get in touch

For general questions, email [support@crackbot.in](mailto:support@crackbot.in). For security reports, email [researcher@crackbot.in](mailto:researcher@crackbot.in).
